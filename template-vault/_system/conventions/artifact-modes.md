# Artifact Modes

Choose Document for asynchronous reading, reference, detailed reasoning and tables; choose Presentation for a live sequential walkthrough and one idea per beat. Both use the same brand and evidence standards. Documents use readable scrolling sections and anchors; presentations use slide state, keyboard/touch navigation and stable slide links. Do not force dense reports into illegible slides. Specify the intended reader, decision and medium before layout. Owner direction overrides defaults. Pricing, confidential data and publishing require the corresponding authorization.
