# Source Attribution

Every factual claim filed carries an inline source: [Source: author or record, channel or URL, YYYY-MM-DD]. Include timezone where event timing matters. A citation must be specific enough to recover the evidence; headings and structural links do not require citations. Direct quotes preserve exact words. Summaries and inference must be labeled accordingly. Owner statements are authoritative for their preferences and reported experience; retain attribution rather than treating every reported external fact as verified. Conflicting sources remain visible with dates and provenance until resolved. Current synthesis never erases the evidence it was based on.
