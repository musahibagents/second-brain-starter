# Presentation Craft

Start with the audience’s decision and one idea per slide. Use evidence-backed numbers and visuals that explain the mechanism. Product demo slides share one application shell with a screen for each workflow; captions stay short. Distinguish a labeled concept mockup from a working product. Use anonymous realistic data in mockups and recorded permission for client material. Derive palette and typography from the relevant brand and maintain contrast; do not inherit another owner’s colors or signatures. Fit a fixed slide canvas proportionally with scale=min(viewportWidth/canvasWidth,viewportHeight/canvasHeight), or use responsive layouts with equivalent legibility. Account for grid columns, spans, gaps and minimum content widths to prevent overflow. Keep slide indexes, navigation, hashes and total counts synchronized. Support keyboard and touch, visible focus and reduced motion. Use explicit animation state and ensure content remains visible if motion fails. Inspect every slide at presentation and mobile sizes. Publication requires explicit authorization; no hosting provider or public-access bypass is assumed.
