# Tiered Enrichment

Use T3 for a justified minimal entity stub, T2 for substantive recurring relevance and T1 for important active relationships/projects with enough evidence for deeper synthesis. Escalate as independent interactions and strategic relevance accumulate, recording why and when. A passing mention does not qualify as an interaction. Do not research or build full assessments for every imported name. T1 subjective sections still require evidence; tier is importance, not confidence. Review over-enriched and neglected important pages during maintenance. Do not automatically delete or archive because a tier changed.
