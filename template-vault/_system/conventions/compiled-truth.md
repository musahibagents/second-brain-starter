# Compiled Truth

Entity pages have two layers: current synthesis above a horizontal separator, dated evidence timeline below it. Rewrite synthesis when facts change rather than appending contradictory paragraphs. Append history; correct earlier mistakes with a new dated entry rather than silently rewriting the event. Every synthesized claim must trace to evidence. Useful sections include state, open threads, assessment and related pages; omit unsupported sections. Assessments require source-type and confidence labels. Event notes and source summaries need not mimic entity structure.
