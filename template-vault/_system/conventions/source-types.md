# Source Types

Label subjective claims about beliefs, motivation, communication and assessment as observed, self-described or inferred. Add confidence and last-evidence date plus a source. Observed describes an actual behavior; self-described records what the person said about themselves; inferred names a deduction and its basis. One incident is not an enduring trait. Confidence depends on quality, independent corroboration and relevance, not forceful wording. Use low for sparse inference, medium for repeated but incomplete evidence, high for directly supported narrow claims. Revisit stale judgments. Record owner corrections with evidence and preserve unresolved external contradictions.
