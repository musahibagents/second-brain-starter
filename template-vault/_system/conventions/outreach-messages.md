# Outreach Messages

Read the owner’s current outreach policy and actual relationship history before drafting. Verify recipient identity, channel, relevance and prior contact. Ground the opening in a specific fact; keep one supported claim and one appropriate next step. Match the owner’s voice and the relationship without inventing familiarity. Avoid fabricated urgency, unsupported results and generic filler. Separate exact draft text, approval, send evidence and reply evidence. Update the contact record only from actual outcomes. Do not publish or transmit from a drafting request. Respect opt-outs and platform restrictions.
