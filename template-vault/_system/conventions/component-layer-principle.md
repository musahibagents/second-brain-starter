# Component Layer Principle

Design content, interaction and continuity together. Content communicates the task; components expose actions and states; continuity helps people stay oriented across sections. Choose meaningful mechanisms such as clear status changes, progress, section navigation or explanatory motion. Motion and decorative effects are optional: evidence, the chosen design system, accessibility and measured performance govern. Avoid adding animation simply to satisfy a quota. Respect reduced motion, keyboard use and touch input. Share reusable primitives and tokens; verify interactions in the rendered interface.
