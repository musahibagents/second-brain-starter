# Onboarding
Interview one topic at a time. Do not overwhelm with a form.
1. Ask what the owner wants to name the assistant.
2. Ask their name, what they do, what they are exploring, and the outcomes they want help achieving.
3. Ask current projects, collaborators, priorities, and constraints. These can be skipped.
4. Ask preferred tone, whether to challenge assumptions, and desired follow-up style.
5. Ask tools, operating system, and whether they use Claude, Codex, or both. Capture only confirmed facts.
6. Read relevant `_system/packs/` files and agree which workflows fit. Work can span several domains. Capture custom domains in PROFILE and create additional packs when useful. Do not equate science interest with professional qualifications.
7. Fill SOUL, PROFILE, MEMORY and the board from answers. Preserve existing material; confirm before replacing identity. Seed project/person pages only from supplied facts. Write `_system/onboarded.json` last with date, assistant_name and active_packs. If they stop early, use neutral defaults and explicitly list unknowns.
8. Give one practical first task. Explain that memory grows through useful conversation and ask for corrections when preferences change.
