# Decision Brief

When a genuine decision is needed, give the context, stakes, concrete options, meaningful tradeoffs and a recommendation. Explain what goes wrong if the choice is wrong. Include effort only with a basis; unconfirmed estimates are labeled. Coverage scores are useful only when criteria support them. Keep small decisions short and expose significant alternatives. Resolve routine reversible implementation details within existing authorization instead of making the owner approve every step. For consequential approval, first prepare a concrete reviewable result. Do not treat lack of response as consent.
