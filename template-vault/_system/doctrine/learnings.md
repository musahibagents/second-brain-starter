# Learnings

Capture discoveries that will save future work, with date, scope, claim, source, type, confidence and relevant paths. Use _system/patterns.md for compiled patterns and their evidence log; no custom database is required. Separate stated intentions from recorded outcomes and do not infer inactivity from missing logs. Three independent dated examples are the default pattern threshold; fewer are watch items. Recheck recency and counterexamples before reuse, and do not transfer a client-specific lesson to all clients without support. A correction supersedes a conclusion while history remains. Promote only durable operating rules to memory. Remove stale rules from active synthesis through a sourced correction, not silent history rewriting.
